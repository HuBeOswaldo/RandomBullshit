# Changelog

## v1.0.4

- Prevented damage from destroying training dummies or dropping their build
  materials. Dismantling still returns the materials normally.
- Removed the "DPS" label prefix and reduced the size of the displayed number.

## v1.0.3

- Added T.W.M. (Training with Mexicanito), a passive training dummy in the
  hammer build menu.
- Added DPS labels above T.W.I.G. and T.W.M., calculated over a rolling
  5-second window.
- Made both training dummies indestructible; they recover to full health after
  5 seconds without damage.
- DPS labels disappear after 5 seconds without a hit.
